# New Thread Handoff

Read `docs/website-decisions.md` first. It is the current implementation source of truth and later explicit decisions supersede older ones.

For Specdest work, also read `website-direction.md`, `architecture.md`, `public/images/README.md`, and `public/images/reference-sources.json` before changing navigation, content structure, visuals, images, or deployment behavior.

Do not infer current production state from an old conversation. Inspect the repository and Firebase configuration before making or describing deployment changes.

## What is reusable for another company
The reusable parts are the process and design principles: problem-first information architecture, selective motion, differentiated menu pages, evidence-led case studies, mobile accordion navigation, optimized local imagery, static prerendering, SEO discipline, and explicit deployment verification.

## What is Specdest-specific
Do not blindly copy Specdest's brand slogan, Japanese copy, menu labels, case studies, company facts, contact details, positioning claims, sky-blue brand color, or exact navigation taxonomy into another company website. Re-derive those from the new company's business, evidence, audience, and brand.

Kinaxis and Conveo are references only. Reuse the reasoning behind hierarchy, editorial rhythm, and selective motion—not their visual identity or content.

When creating another company site from these docs, first separate decisions into: `reusable system rule`, `company-specific content`, and `deployment/repository-specific constraint`.
