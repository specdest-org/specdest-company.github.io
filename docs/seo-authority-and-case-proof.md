## External profile candidates (review before registration)

1. **Clutch** — https://clutch.co/get-listed. Official help: https://help.clutch.co/en/knowledge/get-listed-on-clutch. A free basic profile is available and can show services, company information and approved client reviews. **Important:** Clutch's current basic offer says it does **not** include a website link from directory pages. Do not treat a free Clutch listing as a guaranteed backlink. Complete/claim only with approved factual details; pricing, project minimums and employee counts must be verified.
2. **GoodFirms** — https://www.goodfirms.co/get-listed. Official listing help: https://help.goodfirms.co/how-can-my-business-get-listed-on-goodfirms/. Free submission and research review are available. Check whether a company profile already exists before creating another. Only choose approved service categories, and verify live outbound links if accepted. No guaranteed acceptance or SEO lift.
3. **Google Business Profile** — https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036. **Conditional, not an automatic registration target.** Google requires in-person customer contact during stated hours; online-only businesses and virtual offices without actual eligible operations must not create a listing. Verify eligibility, staffed office or real service-area visits before proceeding.
4. **Direct earned mentions** — Seek consented links from active clients, project partners, professional communities and verifiable published collaborations when they genuinely reference Specdest's role. Prefer a relevant editorial mention over a bulk low-quality directory campaign.
